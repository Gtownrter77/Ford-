## Disclosure and origin rules for app placements

- A sponsor payment, free tool, discount, loaner, or other benefit is a material connection that should be disclosed plainly at the placement itself, such as “Sponsored by [Brand]” or “Paid partner.”
- An unqualified “Made in USA” label should be reserved for a product-level claim substantiated as “all or virtually all” made in the U.S. A U.S. headquarters, domestic factory, or mixed product line is not enough.
- Recommended app treatment: a clearly marked Sponsored / Preferred Brand spotlight may be shown ahead of the normal comparison list. The customer-controlled ranking and the fitment evidence remain available and the sponsorship label stays visible.
