# 4WD Rear Shock Replacement — 2004 Explorer Sport Trac

This teaching workflow is keyed to the app component `rear_shock_absorbers_4wd_3d` and is intended for the 2004 Ford Explorer Sport Trac 4WD V6-4.0L VIN K configuration. The 3D mesh is an inspection aid representing the damper body, piston rod, and mounting eyes; it is not a dimensional substitute for a verified replacement part.

## Safety gates

The workflow stops at the preparation stage unless the student confirms a level work surface, front-wheel chocks, rated jack stands under the frame, and separate support for the rear axle. The vehicle must never be supported by a floor jack alone. The replacement pair must be checked for 4WD fitment, mounting-eye orientation, length, bushings, and hardware before installation.

The app displays the source label with each shock torque record and repeats the safety guidance at the relevant repair steps. It also calls for a post-installation low-speed inspection for contact, loose hardware, oil leakage, abnormal noise, or asymmetric ride height.

## Parts catalog behavior

The linked shopping record is `part_rear_shock_kyb_344269_pair`. Its prices are benchmark comparison values, not a live quote. The catalog explicitly tells the student to verify retailer availability, shipping, warranty, VIN/axle-code application, and pair quantity at checkout. The readiness package `rear_suspension_readiness` also exposes pending fitment checks for mounting hardware and application.
