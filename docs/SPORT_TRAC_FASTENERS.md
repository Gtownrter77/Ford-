# Sport Trac fastener layer

Service-scope bolt + washer + nut stacks on the meter-true hull.
Not a VIN-complete Ford BOM. Hidden TTY head bolts, interior screws, body clips, and harness retainers are not claimed.

## Stacks on the hull

- Wheels: 5 x 1/2-20 lug nuts per corner (OG 84-114 lb-ft) plus an axle/hub nut
- 5R55E: 8 bellhousing joints, 16 pan bolts
- BW4411: 6 adapter joints, 3 shift-motor screws
- Ford 8.8: 10 cover joints + fill plug
- Front axle: 8 cover/flange joints
- 4.0L: 4 mount nuts, 8 intake-flange bolts
- Radiator: 4 bracket joints
- Cab: 6 body mounts
- Bed: 6 T55 floor joints
- Frame rails: 4 body mounts per side

Each joint explodes as its own bolt, washer, and nut.
Torque strings are packaged references. Confirm the workshop manual before use.
