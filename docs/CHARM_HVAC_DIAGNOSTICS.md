## Compressor service (leaf 1705)

FS-10 is assembly-only. Clutch, pulley, field coil, shaft seal are serviceable. Contamination from an internal fail: flush if equipment exists, else filter after the new compressor. New orifice and new accumulator as the flush/filter procedure directs. New accumulator is not required on a clean repair.

PAG: F7AZ-19589-DA (Motorcraft YN-12-C) or equivalent WSH-M1C231-B. Dark oil with normal viscosity is normal on FS-10 (piston-ring carbon).
