# 2004 Sport Trac 4.0L SOHC lean / misfire repair set

Do the cheap vacuum path first. Official WSM covers the PCV valve and intake. The cracked rubber elbow is the community P0171/P0174 failure.

## Parts
- PCV valve: Motorcraft EV-243 (2004 OG). Confirm VIN catalog.
- Hose/elbow: Motorcraft KCV-190 or Ford 4L5Z-6758-HA. Shop: Dorman 48011. Elbow-only: NAPA 9807.
- Plugs: AGSF-22PP, gap 1.3-1.4 mm OG.
- Intake gasket set: VIN catalog, only if lean remains after PCV work.

## Torque (workshop)
- Intake bolts 10 Nm / 89 in-lb (2004 VIN K + CHARM 2005 VIN K)
- Throttle body 9 Nm / 80 in-lb
- Coil bolts 6 Nm / 53 in-lb; bracket 10 Nm / 89 in-lb
- Plugs 20 Nm / 15 lb-ft
- Fuel rail 23 Nm / 17 lb-ft
- PCV elbow: press-on, no torque

## 1. PCV elbow (first)
Job 2 4.0 SOHC, oil fill passenger side. Elbow is rear driver cover to intake. Inspect the UNDERSIDE. If stuck, slice lengthwise, do not score the nipple. Replace KCV-190 if the hose is spongy.

## 2. Official PCV valve (2004 VIN K WSM)
1. Remove vacuum hose.
2. Disconnect coolant hoses from the PCV valve.
3. Unlock CCW and remove from the valve cover.
4. Reverse to install. Lock clockwise. New valve if it came out of the cover.

## 3. Intake gaskets only if Bank 2 stays lean
Battery ground, air box tube, label PCV / booster / EVAP / vacuum, intake bolts, lift manifold, heated PCV fitting while it is up, new gaskets, 89 in-lb middle-out.

## 4. Plugs and coil if misfire remains
Bank 2 driver cyl 4-5-6. Bank 1 passenger 1-2-3. Firing 1-4-2-5-3-6.

## After
Clear codes. Watch STFT and Bank 2 LTFT. Spray the new elbow underside. No foot-pound wrench on 89 in-lb intake bolts.
