OG footnotes that matter:

1. MERCON and MERCON V are not interchangeable. Do not mix.
2. Transmission volumes are approximate dry-fill. Set level on the dipstick.
3. Transfer case: fill to bottom of filler plug hole.
4. Use the coolant type originally equipped.
5. Rear axle fill 6–14 mm (1/4–9/16 in) below filler hole bottom.
6. Synthetic rear-axle lube is lubricated for life unless leak, service, or water submersion.
7. Add 118 ml (4 oz) Additive Friction Modifier XL-7 (EST-M2C118-A) on complete Traction-Lok refill.

## Engine data (4.0L SOHC FFV V6)

- Displacement: 245 cubic inches
- Fuel: 87 octane unleaded or ethanol E85 max
- Firing order: 1-4-2-5-3-6
- Spark plug gap: 1.3–1.4 mm (0.052–0.056 inch)
- Ignition: EDIS
- Compression ratio: 9.7:1

## Dimensions (4-door)

- Overall length: 205.9 in (5230 mm)
- Overall width: 71.8 in (1823 mm)
- Height: 69.9 in / 70.6 in max on 4x4 with 16 in tires
- Wheelbase: 125.9 in (3198 mm)
- Front / rear track: 58.5 in / 58.3 in

## Wheel lug nuts (Roadside Emergencies)

- Bolt size 1/2 x 20
- Torque 113–153 Nm (84–114 lb-ft)
- Retighten at 500 miles / 800 km after any wheel disturbance
