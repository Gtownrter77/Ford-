# Future Affiliate-Link Safeguards

## Purpose

The Part Store may eventually use approved retailer affiliate links to help fund product maintenance and future improvements. This is a **future, optional funding layer**. It is not part of the current private readiness dashboard, and it must never change a recommendation, trigger a purchase, collect payment information, or access a retailer account.

## Customer-facing rules

| Rule | Required behavior |
| --- | --- |
| **Fitment first** | The app must establish the most appropriate part number and show VIN/configuration warnings before it shows any retailer handoff. |
| **Neutral ranking** | “Best value” must be calculated from fitment evidence, delivered total, shipping, core charge, seller condition, availability, warranty, and return terms—not commission amount. |
| **Plain disclosure** | A short, adjacent statement must disclose when a selected outbound retailer link may earn the app a commission. The disclosure must be visible before the link is used. |
| **Customer choice** | Every eligible part must retain non-affiliate/manual retailer links and an “other retailer” path. No retailer is exclusive. |
| **No automatic commerce** | The app must never add an item to a cart, sign in, apply payment, submit an order, or store retailer payment/account information. |
| **Local-first privacy** | Readiness lists, maintenance reminders, and part-watch selections remain in the app. Retailer communication occurs only after a person deliberately opens a retailer handoff. |

## Approved future paths

Amazon Associates describes commission income on qualifying purchases generated through customized affiliate links for eligible publishers and apps. eBay’s Partner Network describes trackable affiliate links that can earn a percentage of qualifying transactions. Both require enrollment, approved program access, and adherence to their terms; neither should be enabled until those approvals exist. [1] [2]

O’Reilly Auto Parts currently describes a creator ambassador program with possible gift cards, merchandise, project funding, and other partnership opportunities. That public page does **not** establish a general auto-parts affiliate commission program, so the Part Store must not claim O’Reilly commission availability unless a direct written program agreement is obtained. [3]

## Disclosure standard

The Federal Trade Commission explains that when a financial connection could affect how people evaluate a recommendation, it should be disclosed clearly and conspicuously. The app should therefore show a concise disclosure directly beside an applicable retailer handoff, for example: **“This retailer link may support future app improvements at no added cost to you. It does not affect fitment or value ranking.”** [4]

## Activation gate

Before any affiliate link is enabled, the product team must confirm program approval, current terms, jurisdictional disclosure requirements, source tracking limits, data retention practices, and an impartial ranking audit. The app should also maintain a visible plain link to the retailer without tracking, so the customer always has a commission-free choice.
